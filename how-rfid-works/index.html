How RFID Works, Component by Component

A passive UHF RFID tag has no battery and no radio transmitter. Instead, it reflects energy back to the reader. The reader sends out a continuous 915 MHz wave. The tag harvests enough of that wave to power a tiny chip. It answers by switching its antenna between two impedances, so the reflection carries its ID back to the reader.
